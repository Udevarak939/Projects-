# Executive Finance Dashboard Specification

## Page 1 — Executive Summary

Cards:
- Revenue
- Revenue vs Budget %
- YoY Growth %
- Gross Margin %
- EBITDA
- EBITDA Margin %

Visuals:
1. Monthly actual vs budget line chart
2. Revenue by region bar chart
3. Gross margin by product/channel
4. Budget variance waterfall
5. Top 5 positive / negative variance drivers

Filters:
- Month
- Region
- Product
- Channel
- Customer segment

## Page 2 — Performance & Drivers

Visuals:
- Revenue trend by region
- Gross margin trend
- Opex as % of revenue
- Variance decomposition by region/product/channel
- Transaction volume and AOV

Drill-through:
Executive -> Region -> Product -> Channel

## Page 3 — Forecast & Scenario Planning

Visuals:
- 3-month rolling revenue forecast
- Base / Upside / Downside EBITDA comparison
- Scenario assumptions table
- Sensitivity matrix for price and volume

## Business narrative

The dashboard should answer three questions in under two minutes:

1. What happened?
2. Why did it happen?
3. What should the business watch next?

Use tooltips and drill-throughs to avoid overcrowding the executive view.
