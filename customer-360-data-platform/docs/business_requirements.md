# Business Requirements

## Objective
Create a governed Customer 360 analytics platform that combines order, customer, product, payment and delivery data to support revenue, retention and operational decisions.

## Stakeholder questions
- Which customer segments generate the most lifetime revenue?
- What is the repeat purchase rate by cohort?
- Which sellers/categories have delivery issues?
- Which customers show low-engagement risk?
- What revenue trends should guide planning?

## Core KPIs
Revenue, orders, average order value, repeat purchase rate, customer lifetime value, delivery days, cancellation rate and customer segment.

## Functional requirements
1. Ingest source data reproducibly.
2. Validate schema and data quality before transformation.
3. Produce reusable silver and gold datasets.
4. Publish dimensional warehouse models.
5. Provide BI-ready KPI marts.
6. Provide customer segmentation and churn-risk analytics.
