# Real-Time Marketing Campaign & A/B Testing Analytics

End-to-end portfolio project for campaign performance, funnel analytics, A/B testing, customer segments, CAC and ROAS.

## Stack
Python, Pandas, NumPy, SciPy, SQL, PostgreSQL, Kafka concepts, Streamlit, Plotly.

## Workflow
1. Generate campaign events.
2. Validate and transform events.
3. Aggregate impressions, clicks and purchases into funnel KPIs.
4. Compare control vs treatment using a two-proportion test.
5. Calculate uplift, confidence interval, CAC and ROAS.
6. Segment results by channel and customer cohort.
7. Publish an executive dashboard.

## Portfolio outcome
Demonstrates experiment design, statistical reasoning, marketing analytics and business communication—not just dashboard building.
